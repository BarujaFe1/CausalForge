# CausalForge — Portfolio Pitch

## One-liner
CausalForge is an applied causal inference lab that helps teams evaluate whether a business intervention created impact — or only looked like it did.

## Interview talking points
- Correlation vs causation and why dashboards alone mislead decisions
- Diff-in-differences and matching as transparent MVP estimators
- Assumption checklists, uncertainty intervals and declared limitations
- Responsible analytics: never promise automatic causality
- Full-stack delivery: Next.js + FastAPI + synthetic controlled examples

## LinkedIn / portfolio blurb
Built CausalForge to move beyond didactic A/B tooling into observational causal evaluation for campaigns, ops changes and product interventions — with explicit hypotheses, uncertainty and decision memos.
